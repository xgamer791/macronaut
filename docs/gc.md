# Garmin Connect — feature inventory

Pulled 3 September 2026. Sources: [App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/garmin-connect/id583446403), Garmin’s [May 2026 Connect blog](https://www.garmin.com/en-US/blog/fitness/unlocking-the-potential-of-garmin-connect/), and the current Connect vs Connect+ split ([Garmin Wiki](https://wiki.garminrumors.com/Garmin_Connect)). Mobbin’s screen gallery was behind login, so this is a product inventory, not a pixel audit.

Almost everything below is **hardware-dependent**. The phone app is the dashboard. The watch or band is what records it.

Free Connect still has all core health metrics, workouts, Coach plans, maps, standard LiveTrack, badges, Lifestyle Logging, and Essentials. Garmin’s line is that nothing that was free moved behind Connect+ ($6.99/mo or $69.99/yr).

## Home

- Customizable **Home** with **In Focus** tiles and **At a Glance** cards
- **Essentials** row: up to 4 pinned stats (Sleep Score, Body Battery, Training Readiness, HRV, Health Status, Steps)
- Today + last 7 days on cards; tap a card for history/charts
- Latest activity / planned workouts
- Events, training plans, challenges
- **Morning Report**
- **Insights** (free); **Active Intelligence** (Connect+)

## Activities

- Sync and archive workouts from Garmin devices
- Detail: time, distance, laps, elevation, pace/speed, heart rate, power, cadence
- GPS map with metric polyline
- Zone breakdowns, splits, personal records, mileage history
- Auto-detected activities (Move IQ)
- Activity calendar
- Compare / historical reports (deeper on web)
- **LiveTrack** (share a live activity); **GroupTrack**
- **Live Activity** on the phone during an indoor workout (Connect+)
- GPX / TCX import and export
- Gear tracking (shoes, bikes, mileage)

## Health and recovery

- Steps, floors, intensity minutes, calories (active / total)
- Heart rate (resting, all-day, zones)
- **HRV Status**
- Stress
- **Body Battery**
- Sleep: duration, stages, REM, **Sleep Score**, **Sleep Coach**
- Naps
- **Pulse Ox** (sleep / all-day, where legal)
- Respiration
- Skin temperature
- Hydration logging
- Weight (Garmin Index scales)
- Blood pressure (Index BPM)
- **Health Snapshot**
- **Health Status** (baseline-deviation alerts on nightly metrics)
- **Lifestyle Logging** (caffeine, alcohol, cold plunge, etc., with correlations)
- Menstrual cycle: log, predictions, symptoms
- Pregnancy: insights, kick counts, blood sugar log
- Wheelchair / push tracking (supported devices)

## Training

- Workout builder: run, bike, swim, strength, cardio, yoga, pilates
- Push workouts to the watch
- **Garmin Coach**: run, bike, triathlon, strength (adaptive plans)
- PacePro pacing
- Training Status, Training Effect, Training Load, Recovery Time
- **Training Readiness**
- Lactate threshold, FTP, VO2 Max, Fitness Age, race predictor
- Calendar / race events
- TrainingPeaks / TrainerRoad plan import
- Connect+ coaching videos for Run / Cycling / Triathlon Coach

## Maps and routes

- Course creation, sync to device
- Segments + leaderboards
- Heatmaps / popularity routing
- **Garmin Trails** search (free); save + push to device (Connect+)
- **3D maps** on activities/courses/trails (Connect+)

## Nutrition (Connect+, January 2026)

- Food + macro logging
- Global food database
- Barcode scan
- AI camera (multi-item plate/shelf)
- Burned vs consumed
- Watch glance + voice logging
- Active Intelligence nutrition insights
- Replaces MyFitnessPal sync for subscribers

## Social

- Connections (follow friends / athletes)
- Newsfeed
- Step / intensity / badge challenges
- Badges + points + badge leaderboard
- Exclusive badges/challenges (Connect+)
- Profile frames (Connect+)

## Device

- Pair / unpair, firmware
- Watch settings sync
- Phone notifications
- Music management (supported watches)
- **Connect IQ** (watch faces, apps, data fields)
- Garmin Pay setup
- Safety / Assistance / incident detection
- Find my watch
- Weather

## Reports and web extras

- Weekly fitness email / reports
- Configurable exports and long-range trends (web is deeper)
- Custom charting, lap compare, print
- **Performance Dashboard** — 120+ chart templates (Connect+)
- **Connect Rundown** — AI period summary (Connect+)

## Sync

- Apple Health
- Google Health Connect
- Strava
- MyFitnessPal (legacy; nutrition is first-party on Connect+)
- TrainingPeaks, TrainerRoad
- Other third-party uploads (Komoot, etc.)

## Connect+ only

- Active Intelligence
- Performance Dashboard
- Live Activity
- Expanded LiveTrack
- Coaching videos
- Exclusive badges
- Trails save/push
- 3D maps
- Rundown
- Nutrition

## Not Garmin Connect

Separate Garmin apps: Connect IQ Store, Garmin Golf, Garmin Dive, Tacx Training, Garmin Jr., Explore, Messenger, Drive.

## Macronaut takeaway

Steal **Home customization** (Today already has two hero modules), **tap-a-card → history**, and **workout-first activity detail**. Do not clone the rest. Most of this list is a watch OS, not an app.
